# Open the admin on your phone

Open `/staff` on the café website. It sends you to `/admin/`. Sign in with your own account. The admin checks your role before it shows any café records.

## Stay signed in

- On your own phone, select **Keep me signed in on this device** before you sign in. The browser saves your session between visits, and the sign-in library renews it while the app is active.
- On a shared phone or computer, leave the box off. Your session stays in the current tab. Use **Sign out** when you finish.
- The browser remembers your choice. Signing out clears the saved session from both kinds of browser storage. If you share the device, uncheck the box the next time you sign in.

## Android with Chrome

1. Open `/admin/` in Chrome.
2. Tap the three dots, then **Add to home screen**. Choose **Install** if Chrome offers it. Some versions offer **Create shortcut**.
3. Open **CDL Admin** from your home screen and sign in there. [Chrome instructions](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/9658361?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid)

## iPhone with Safari

1. Open `/admin/` in Safari.
2. Open **Share**, then tap **Add to Home Screen**.
3. If Safari shows **Open as Web App**, turn it on. Tap **Add**.
4. Open the icon and sign in there if prompted. [Apple instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/open-as-web-app-iphea86e5236/ios)

The icon needs a network connection. Phone installation and storage behavior on physical devices remain **UNVERIFIED**.
