# Owner confirmation checklist

- Confirm the café name and tagline and provide the approved logo and photography rights.
- Resolve the Vikas Khand versus Vipul Khand address and approve the map pin.
- Confirm the café phone, domain email, social links and WhatsApp Business number.
- Set actual opening hours and booking slots, and choose how many reservations a slot can hold.
- Supply dishes, descriptions, prices, dietary information, allergen wording and availability. Add images and switch off the sample label once approved.
- Confirm which requests to accept, delivery area, fees, minimum order, tax and payment arrangements.
- Confirm vacancies, CV retention and access. Configure the owner first, then create staff Auth accounts and add only the people who need access.
- Complete the smoke checks on the new project before enabling real customer submissions.
