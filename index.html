Our Story

Founded in 2023, Café De Leila was born from a simple belief: great food brings people together. Nestled in the heart of Gomti Nagar, Lucknow, we've been serving authentic flavors with a touch of magic ever since.

Our chefs blend traditional recipes with contemporary techniques, using only the freshest ingredients sourced locally. From our signature Butter Chicken to handcrafted pizzas and South Indian classics, every dish tells a story.

Whether you're here for a quick lunch, a family dinner, or a special celebration, we promise an experience that delights all your senses.
